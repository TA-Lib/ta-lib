# SUB

## Summary

Element-wise subtraction of two input series.

## Formula

outReal[i] = inReal0[i] - inReal1[i]

## Inputs

- `inReal0` — Minuend series
- `inReal1` — Subtrahend series

## Outputs

- `outReal` — Per-element difference inReal0 - inReal1

## Aliases

Subtract, Vector Subtraction
